Welcome. You may be here because something has become difficult to sort through on your own, or because you want more clarity, ease, or depth in your life and relationships. Whatever brings you here, I’d be glad to hear more about it and see whether working together might be useful. I work with individuals, couples, and families.

My style is warm, curious, and direct. I try to create enough room for honesty, including the parts of your experience that may feel difficult to say out loud. Clients have described me as steady and grounded, especially when conversations feel difficult or emotionally charged.

You can find more information about my fees and practice at my Psychology Today profile.
